Why Miss K built this website

🏫

Built for BASIS Bangkok middle schoolers

Our courses move fast and cover a lot. This site was made for our students in Grades 6–8 — the same courses, the same units and the same learning objectives you meet in class, in one place you can open at school or at home.

🎯

Every learning objective, covered

Each learning objective in Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Math has its own simulation. Missed a lesson? Need a second look before a test? Find the objective, explore it, practice it and check yourself.

🎮

Learning should feel like play

When something is fun, we come back to it — and practice is how ideas stick. So every finished lab earns XP. XP raises your level, unlocks badges, buys avatars and backgrounds in the store, and puts you on the monthly leaderboard.

💡

See it, then understand it

Instead of only reading a formula, you move a slider and watch what changes. Each lab follows the same path — mission, simulation, steps, everyday example, practice, check and reflection — so you always know what to do next.

How to earn XP

🎯+15Complete the mission
✏️+20Get 3 practice questions right
✅+10 to +30Take the Check (+5 per correct answer)
💭+15Write your reflection
🏁+20Bonus: finish all four parts

Up to 100 XP per lab. Each part pays once; retaking the Check only pays if you beat your best score. Spending XP in the store never lowers your rank — the leaderboard counts XP earned.

For families

Everything is free and needs no login. Progress is saved in the browser on this device. Students can download a work file for Miss K from inside any lab, and an XP card from My profile. The leaderboard shows only a nickname, an avatar and a grade — never a full name.
